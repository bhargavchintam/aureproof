**Reviewer.** The lead maintainer is also the reviewer (decided 2026-10-29,
ADR-0005 amendment). Reviews are self-reviews and are recorded as such: a review
comment on the issue, and the role `author` in any record that names reviewers.
Nothing in the repository is described as independently reviewed. Cadence: one
review pass per milestone gate, before the gate is recorded.

Conduct reports go to the address in [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

A full `GOVERNANCE.md` and a maintainer ladder are deferred until the first
regular external contributor appears (plan v2 §9.2).
